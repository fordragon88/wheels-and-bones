Wheels & Bones is a free educational game about wheelchair repair. Martin, a kind zombie, runs a repair workshop in a fantasy town. Diagnose the problem, pick the right tools and fix real wheelchair faults: flats, caster flutter, wheel locks, spokes, bearings, joysticks, batteries, seat elevators and more. Repair steps and facts are based on manufacturers' manuals collected by the Bay Area Repair Coalition (BARC).

The game loads in about 20–40 seconds the first time (8 MB). It runs in the browser, no installation needed. Progress is saved in your browser. Open the game on its own page for a bigger view.

How to play

  • Mouse: click parts of the wheelchair to inspect them, choose a diagnosis, then pick a tool and click the part to fix.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move between items, Tab jumps between groups, Enter or Space selects, Esc goes back. F1 shows all keys.

Accessibility

  • The whole game can be played with the keyboard only.
  • Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) read the game: what is on screen, every choice, hints and the characters' lines. Click the game or move focus into it; if your screen reader does not pass keys to the game, switch to focus mode (NVDA: Insert+Space).
  • No screen reader? Press F2 and the browser reads the game aloud.
  • R repeats where you are and what to do, T reads the whole screen, H gives a hint.
  • Settings (gear icon): high-contrast background, instant text, volume.
  • To leave the game with the keyboard, press F6 or Ctrl+L to go to the address bar.
